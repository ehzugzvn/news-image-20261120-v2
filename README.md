# Global Briefing — Friday 20 November 2026

A one-page infographic briefing that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image: a navy-and-amber GLOBAL BRIEFING sheet with a dot-matrix world map and 14 numbered pins for the day's biggest stories, a row of headline stat chips, a UTC timeline rail, three illustrated lead stories (the Kyiv barrage, Cyclone Akvan and the COP31 deadlock in Antalya), and 40 reported stories grouped by region — Europe, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, Africa, the Americas and Science — every figure exactly as reported that day.
